The technology

How it works

Your WiFi is constantly painting a spatial picture of every room. Sentinel reads it. A person's body subtly disturbs the radio waves passing through it — and those disturbances encode movement, breathing, and where someone is.

Sense

Small ceiling/wall nodes read the radio-signal disturbance (WiFi Channel State Information) caused by people moving and breathing — through walls, in the dark, no line of sight.

Locate

A mesh of nodes triangulates where motion is, so each signal is attributed to the right room and the right person — backed by a tiny coin-cell tag where certainty matters.

Learn

The system learns each resident's personal baseline — vitals, sleep rhythm, and the unique signature of their gait — getting more accurate the longer it runs.

Act

Drift from normal, a fall, a help gesture, or an unidentified person triggers the right response — paged to staff, logged for compliance, narrated in plain language.

Security & intruder detection

You can't hide from a signal the way you hide from a camera

Cameras have blind spots, dark corners, and angles to duck. Radio sensing fills the whole volume of a room — there is no frame to step out of. An unrecognised presence is seen the moment it moves.

  • 1

    Unidentified presence, flagged

    A person whose gait the system doesn't recognise — and who carries no staff badge — is flagged as unidentified, located to a room in real time.
  • 2

    After-hours intrusion alerts

    An unknown person in a resident's room out of hours raises an immediate security alert, with the location pinpointed for responding staff.
  • 3

    Nowhere to hide

    Through walls, in total darkness, around corners — the sensing field doesn't depend on light or line of sight. There's no blind spot to exploit.

Identity by movement

It learns how each resident moves

Everyone walks differently. Sentinel learns each resident's gait — their pace, stride, and rhythm — and builds a movement signature unique to them. Over time it can identify residents fairly accurately from how they move, with no tag, no camera, and nothing to wear.

That signature does two jobs at once: it keeps the right vitals and history attached to the right person — and it makes anyone whose gait isn't recognised stand out instantly.
