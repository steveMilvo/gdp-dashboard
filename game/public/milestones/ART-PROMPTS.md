# Image-generation prompts — milestone vignettes, buildings & equipment

## A. Milestone clipping vignettes (drop into THIS folder)

These appear inside the "MILDURA CULTIVATOR — EXTRA" newspaper banners.
**Format: JPEG, landscape 5:3 (e.g. 1000×600). No text, no frame, no border.**
Save with the exact filename shown.

**Shared style (prepend to every prompt):**
Painted video-game event illustration, Age of Empires II Definitive Edition
style, richly detailed digital oil painting, warm natural light, painterly
brushwork, muted period-authentic colours, landscape composition with a clear
single subject. No text, no frame, no border.

- **1830.jpg — Sturt's whaleboat:** A wooden whaleboat with a small sail and
  six men rowing down a wide brown river between banks lined with massive river
  red gum trees, an officer in navy uniform standing at the stern with a chart,
  golden morning light, Australia 1830.

- **1847.jpg — The squatter run:** An 1840s Australian sheep station on a dry
  plain — a low timber-slab homestead with bark roof and a long woolshed, a
  flock of merino sheep raising dust, stockmen on horseback, river red gums and
  the Murray River glinting behind.

- **1887.jpg — Surveying the township:** Surveyors with a theodolite and chain
  pegging out a wide dirt avenue on red-soil scrubland, wooden survey pegs and
  flags in neat lines to the horizon, tents and drays, a young engineer in a
  bowler hat pointing at plans, 1887.

- **1889.jpg — Psyche Bend Pump Station:** A Victorian brick pump house with a
  tall smoking chimney stack on a riverbank, huge iron pipes rising from the
  river up the bank, white steam, water gushing into an irrigation channel,
  engineers in waistcoats, 1889.

- **1891.jpg — Rio Vista:** A grand Queen-Anne style two-storey villa of warm
  red brick with white timber fretwork verandas, tower and iron-lace details,
  formal garden with a fountain, overlooking a wide river at golden hour, 1891.

- **1893.jpg — The Crash:** A crowd of worried townsfolk in 1890s dress
  gathered outside a closed colonial bank, CLOSED shutters on the doors, a man
  reading a newspaper, muted grey light, anxious mood. (No readable text.)

- **1895.jpg — The Fruit Republic:** Long timber drying racks covered in
  sultana grapes in golden rows, workers carrying wooden trays, a corrugated
  iron packing shed, vineyards behind, bright optimistic sunlight, 1895.

## C. What images canNOT do

The in-world 3D buildings must be modelled (GLTF) — 2D paintings can't be
placed as structures. These images serve as banners, cards, codex plates, and
as concept reference for the 3D modelling pass.
