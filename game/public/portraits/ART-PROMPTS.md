# Image-generation prompts for the four persona portraits

Generate each portrait with the shared style block + its subject block, then
save as JPEG (3:4 portrait, ≥512×680, ideally 1024×1360) with the exact
filename shown. No frame, border, or text in the image — the game draws its
own gold frame and nameplate.

## Shared style (prepend to every prompt)

Painted video-game character portrait, Age of Empires II Definitive Edition
style, richly detailed digital oil painting, warm natural light, head-and-torso
composition facing slightly off-centre, realistic proportions, muted
period-authentic colours, fine brushwork detail on fabric and skin, soft
painterly background with depth. Portrait orientation 3:4, no frame, no
border, no text, no nameplate.
