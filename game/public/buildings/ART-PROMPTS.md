# Image-generation prompts — historic buildings, monuments & clubs (codex/build cards)

**Format: JPEG, square 1:1, ~600×600 or larger. No text, no frame, no border.**
Save into this folder with the exact filename shown. These become build-menu
cards and codex plates (slots arrive with the construction-menu milestone);
they also serve as concept reference for the 3D models.

**Shared style (prepend to every prompt):**
Painted video-game building portrait, Age of Empires II Definitive Edition
style, richly detailed digital oil painting, warm natural light, three-quarter
view of a single building as the clear subject, period-authentic 1890s-1920s
Australian country town, dusty wide street, painterly brushwork. No text, no
frame, no border.

All facts per docs/mildura-history.md. Context worth keeping in mind: the
colony was founded "dry" (temperance), so the coffee palace and members' clubs
were the town's real social centres.

- **grand_coffee_palace.jpg — Mildura Grand Coffee Palace (1889):** A grand
  three-storey Victorian coffee palace hotel with arched verandas and iron
  lace balconies on a wide dusty avenue, horse-drawn buggies out front,
  temperance-era elegance. (Later the Grand Hotel.)

- **mildura_club.jpg — The Mildura Club (1894):** A dignified single-storey
  Victorian gentlemen's club on a corner block — red brick, white columned
  porch, manicured hedge, brass plate by the door, leather-and-cigars British
  club character in an Australian country town.

- **settlers_club.jpg — The Settlers Club (1895):** A modest early clubhouse:
  timber and corrugated iron, wide veranda, men in waistcoats and hats
  gathered around a makeshift bar of two boards laid across barrels, warm
  lamplight, pioneer conviviality.

- **workingmans_club.jpg — Mildura Working Man's Club (1894):** A long
  low-slung brick club building on Deakin Avenue; through the open doors an
  immensely long polished timber bar stretches away with a row of beer pulls
  (it later held the world's-longest-bar record), workers in shirtsleeves.

- **langtree_hall.jpg — Langtree Hall (1889):** Mildura's first public hall —
  a simple gabled Victorian civic hall of brick and timber with arched
  windows and a small porch, posters by the door, site of the 1896 Royal
  Commission hearings.

- **carnegie_library.jpg — Carnegie Free Library (1908):** A neat Edwardian
  free library of red brick with cream trim and a small tower, arched entry,
  citizens in 1900s dress on the steps. (One of only four Carnegie libraries
  in Victoria, funded by Andrew Carnegie's £2,000 grant.)

- **clock_tower.jpg — Memorial Clock Tower (1921):** A WWI memorial clock
  tower with a four-faced clock rising from the corner of the Carnegie
  building, honour-roll wreaths at its base, dedicated 1921.

- **sacred_heart.jpg — Sacred Heart Peace Memorial Church (1921):** A red
  brick Romanesque parish church with a rose window and arched entrance,
  built as a WWI peace memorial, gum trees beside, morning light.

- **chaffey_statue.jpg — W.B. Chaffey statue:** A life-size bronze statue of
  a Victorian gentleman in a suit on a white marble pedestal in a landscaped
  centre plantation of a wide avenue, palm trees behind. (Sculptor Paul
  Montford.)

- **cenotaph.jpg — Mildura Cenotaph:** A white marble WWI digger soldier
  statue standing at rest on a tall plinth in the avenue's centre plantation,
  wreaths at the base, sombre and proud.

- **travelling_fountain.jpg — The Chaffey fountain (the "travelling"
  fountain):** An ornate Victorian cast-iron garden fountain with tiered
  bowls, photographed-style in two-life composition: playing in a grand villa
  garden. (In-game this asset moves: Rio Vista garden 1891 → idle after 1897 →
  King George V Memorial Fountain on Deakin Avenue 1936.)

- **paddle_steamer.jpg — Paddle steamer at the wharf:** A Murray River paddle
  steamer with tall black funnel and side wheel moored at a timber wharf
  stacked with wool bales, deckhands loading, river red gums behind.

- **psyche_pumps.jpg — Psyche Bend pump interior:** The great triple-expansion
  steam pumping engine — gleaming brass and green-painted iron, spinning
  flywheel, engineers with oil cans, shafts of light through arched windows.
