# Image-generation prompts — historic buildings, monuments & clubs (codex/build cards)

**Format: JPEG, square 1:1, ~600×600 or larger. No text, no frame, no border.**
Save into this folder with the exact filename shown. These become build-menu
cards and codex plates (slots arrive with the construction-menu milestone);
they also serve as concept reference for the 3D models.

Each entry lists a **visual reference lead** — check it before generating so
the art matches the real building. Facts per docs/mildura-history.md.
Context: the colony was founded "dry" (temperance), so the coffee palace and
members' clubs were the town's real social centres.

**Shared style (prepend to every prompt):**
Painted video-game building portrait, Age of Empires II Definitive Edition
style, richly detailed digital oil painting, warm natural light, three-quarter
view of a single building as the clear subject, period-authentic 1890s-1920s
Australian country town, dusty wide street, painterly brushwork. No text, no
frame, no border.

- **grand_coffee_palace.jpg — Mildura Grand Coffee Palace (1889):** A grand
  late-Victorian coffee palace hotel — wide two-level verandas with timber and
  iron-lace balcony detailing, pale roof, arched openings, on a wide dusty
  avenue with horse-drawn buggies. Temperance-era elegance, NOT a modern
  hotel. *Refs: https://thechaffeytrail.com.au/cool_timeline/1889/ ·
  https://beresford.com.au/history/Mildura.htm*

- **mildura_club.jpg — The Mildura Club (clubhouse 1920):** ⚠️ Date care: the
  club was (re-)established 1894 but its purpose-built clubhouse (cnr Deakin
  Ave & Ninth St) was completed Dec 1919 / opened 1920 — render the **1920
  building**: dignified red-brick club with white columned porch, manicured
  hedge, brass plate, British-club character. (An 1894-era scene would be
  earlier, humbler premises instead.) *Ref:
  https://www.milduraclub.com.au/club-history*

- **settlers_club.jpg — The Settlers Club (1895):** A modest pioneer
  clubhouse — a small three-roomed timber cottage (its first premises were in
  Orange Avenue), gabled roof, corrugated-iron veranda, picket fence; inside
  glimpse of the first "bar": two boards laid across a row of barrels, men in
  waistcoats, warm lamplight. *Ref (c.1906 postcard, State Library Victoria):
  https://viewer.slv.vic.gov.au/?entity=IE1446394*

- **workingmans_club.jpg — Mildura Working Man's Club (est. 1894):** A long
  low brick club building on Deakin Avenue. ⚠️ The world-famous bar came
  later: T-shaped bar 1938, rebuilt 1970 at ~91 m (298 ft 7½ in) with 32 beer
  taps — if showing the legendary bar interior, that's the mid-century asset,
  not 1894. *Ref: https://www.milduraworkers.com.au/photo-gallery*

- **langtree_hall.jpg — Langtree Hall (1889):** Mildura's first public hall —
  simple, civic, functional and frontier-town in character: brick and timber,
  gabled, arched windows, small porch, posters by the door. Hosted much of
  the 1896 Royal Commission. *Ref:
  https://thechaffeytrail.com.au/the-sites/langtree-hall/*

- **carnegie_library.jpg — Carnegie Free Library (1908):** A neat Edwardian
  free library of red brick with cream trim, arched entry, its own **small
  original tower** — ⚠️ do NOT include the tall war-memorial clock tower
  (that replaced this tower in 1921 and is a separate asset). Citizens in
  1900s dress on the steps. *Ref:
  https://www.waymarking.com/waymarks/wm145X3_Carnegie_Library_Mildura_Victoria*

- **clock_tower.jpg — Memorial Clock Tower (1921):** The WWI memorial clock
  tower that replaced the Carnegie Library's original tower: four-faced clock
  imported from England, sandstone/cream civic tower with arched base,
  honour wreaths, dedicated 1921. *Ref:
  https://www.monumentaustralia.org/search/display/32595-memorial-clock-tower*

- **sacred_heart.jpg — Sacred Heart Church (1921):** The original red-brick
  Lombardic-Romanesque Catholic church as at its first Mass (18 Dec 1921) —
  rose window, arched entrance, gum trees, morning light. ⚠️ Render the 1921
  massing only; the "Peace Memorial" renaming and extensions came in 1969.
  *Ref:
  https://www.churchesaustralia.org/list-of-churches/denominations/catholic/directory/5617-sacred-heart-peace-memorial-catholic-church*

- **chaffey_statue.jpg — W.B. Chaffey statue:** Life-size bronze gentleman in
  a suit on a pale marble pedestal in the Deakin Avenue centre plantation —
  palms and gums behind, civic buildings beyond. (Sculptor Paul Montford.)
  *Ref: https://www.monumentaustralia.org/search/display/32613*

- **cenotaph.jpg — Mildura Cenotaph:** White stone WWI digger soldier
  standing at rest on a tall plinth, wreaths at the base, solemn
  centre-plantation civic setting (Deakin Ave / Henderson Park feel). *Ref:
  https://placesofpride.awm.gov.au/memorials/159441*

- **travelling_fountain.jpg — The Chaffey fountain (the "travelling"
  fountain):** An ornate Victorian cast-iron garden fountain with tiered
  bowls playing in Rio Vista's formal garden. Historically grounded mechanic:
  original at Rio Vista → relocated to Deakin Avenue 1936 as the King George V
  Memorial Fountain (Rio Vista now has a replica). *Ref:
  https://thechaffeytrail.com.au/the-sites/rio-vista/*

- **paddle_steamer.jpg — Paddle steamer at Mildura wharf:** A Murray River
  paddle steamer — tall black funnel, side wheel — moored at a timber wharf
  stacked with wool bales, deckhands loading, river red gums behind.
  (Public-domain 1905/06 Mildura steamer photos exist.) *Ref:
  https://picryl.com/topics/paddle+steamers+of+the+murray+river*

- **psyche_pumps.jpg — Psyche Bend pump interior:** The triple-expansion
  steam pumping engine, rendered to the heritage photos: **green-painted
  Tangye machinery, red-rimmed flywheel, white pipes**, brass and black steel
  details, arched windows, high timber-and-iron roof, engineers with oil
  cans, shafts of light. *Refs:
  https://vhd.heritage.vic.gov.au/search/nattrust_result_detail/68948 ·
  https://thechaffeytrail.com.au/the-sites/psyche-bend-pump-station/*
